\section{Closure tests}\label{sec:closure}
Apply the final SF map to a validation data/MC comparison that did not determine the corresponding SF bins, for example a disjoint run or event subset, or a suitably independent control region. Keep the data selection, MC corrections and background treatment otherwise unchanged. Show data and MC before and after SF application, and the data/MC ratios, for representative lepton $p_{\mathrm T}$ and $\eta$, jet multiplicity and missing momentum. State the normalization convention and any use of the same data in map derivation. Replotting the efficiency used to construct Eq.~\eqref{eq:sf} is an algebraic closure but does not independently validate the correction. If no independent sample or region is available, rename this section ``Application cross-checks'' and disclose the limited independence of the comparison.
