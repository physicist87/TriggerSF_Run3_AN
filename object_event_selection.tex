\section{Object and event selection}\label{sec:selection}
The offline selection used for the trigger-efficiency measurement uses the Run 3 configuration described below.
It is distinct from the final CPV analysis selection used for the final event sample.
In particular, the trigger study uses a subleading-lepton threshold of 15~GeV and a missing-transverse-momentum threshold of 100~GeV.
The final CPV analysis requirements of at least two jets, a same-flavour $Z$ veto, and a same-flavour missing-momentum threshold of 40~GeV are not substituted for the trigger-study selection.
The 2025 extension uses the same object and event selection as the 2024 measurement.
Year-dependent calibrations and the implemented trigger configuration remain subject to verification against the production code.

\subsection{Electron selection}\label{sec:electron}
Electrons satisfy $p_{\mathrm T}>15~\mathrm{GeV}$ and $|\eta_{\mathrm{SC}}|<2.4$, where $\eta_{\mathrm{SC}}$ is the pseudorapidity of the associated ECAL supercluster.
Electrons in the barrel--endcap transition region, $1.4442<|\eta_{\mathrm{SC}}|<1.566$, are excluded.
The tight cut-based electron identification working point is required.
Electron identification scale factors and the corresponding momentum corrections are applied according to the production configuration.

\subsection{Muon selection}\label{sec:muon}
Muons satisfy $p_{\mathrm T}>15~\mathrm{GeV}$ and $|\eta|<2.4$.
Global muons passing the recommended tight muon identification are selected, with PF-based relative isolation below 0.15.
Muon identification and isolation scale factors and the corresponding momentum corrections are applied according to the production configuration.

\subsection{Jet selection and veto map}\label{sec:jet}
Jets are clustered with the anti-$k_{\mathrm T}$ algorithm with radius $R=0.4$ from PUPPI-weighted particle-flow candidates.
Selected analysis jets satisfy $p_{\mathrm T}>30~\mathrm{GeV}$, $|\eta|<2.4$, and the tight lepton-veto jet identification.
Jets within $\Delta R<0.4$ of a selected lepton are removed.
Jet energy corrections and jet energy resolution corrections follow the period-dependent production configuration.

The Jet Veto Map is applied as an event veto.
Its jet eligibility threshold is $p_{\mathrm T}>15~\mathrm{GeV}$, with tight lepton-veto jet identification and the recommended charged and neutral electromagnetic-fraction requirements.
This threshold is separate from the 30~GeV threshold used to select analysis jets.
An event is rejected if an eligible jet falls in a vetoed region.

\subsection{Missing transverse momentum}\label{sec:met}
PUPPI missing transverse momentum is used, with $p_{\mathrm T}^{\mathrm{miss}}>100~\mathrm{GeV}$ required in all three channels.
The same offline requirement is imposed on the efficiency numerator and denominator.
Jet energy scale and resolution corrections are propagated to the missing transverse momentum.

\subsection{Event selection and corrections}\label{sec:events}
Events contain at least two oppositely charged selected leptons in the $ee$, $e\mu$, or $\mu\mu$ channel.
The leading and subleading leptons satisfy $p_{\mathrm T}>25$ and $15~\mathrm{GeV}$, respectively, and the dilepton invariant mass satisfies $m_{\ell\ell}>20~\mathrm{GeV}$.
At least one selected jet passes the medium UParT working point, and $p_{\mathrm T}^{\mathrm{miss}}>100~\mathrm{GeV}$ is required.
The documented trigger-study selection does not specify an additional minimum of two jets or a same-flavour $Z$-mass veto.
An exact-two-lepton requirement is likewise not inferred from the stated requirement of at least two leptons.

The event-quality flags documented for the study are listed in Table~\ref{tab:filters}.
For the 2024 simulation, the pileup reweighting was updated to match the C--I data-taking periods rather than B--I.
Lepton identification and isolation scale factors and the object corrections are applied consistently with the measurement configuration.
The measured trigger scale factor is not applied when deriving the simulated trigger efficiency itself.
The same non-trigger MC weights are used in the efficiency numerator and denominator.
