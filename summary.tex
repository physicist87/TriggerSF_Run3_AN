\section{Summary}\label{sec:summary}
The Run 3 dilepton trigger efficiency will be measured in MET-reference data and dileptonic $t\bar t$ simulation under the nominal CPV offline selection. The data-to-simulation ratio will be provided by period and channel as a two-dimensional lepton-$p_{\mathrm T}$ scale-factor map with its uncertainty. Correlation studies test the reference-trigger assumption, and independent closure tests assess the applied correction. \insertitem{Brief quantitative conclusion after maps and uncertainties are available.}
